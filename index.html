About Ananya Fashion

Ananya Fashion is a registered textile and garment manufacturing company focused on providing contract-based production, wholesale supply and retail-ready supply solutions to established textile, apparel and clothing businesses.

The company works with clients who need products manufactured to defined specifications, quality requirements, quantities and agreed per-piece or per-unit commercial terms. Customers may optionally provide a reference sample. Our business approach is focused on clear communication, requirement review and production planning that supports long-term partnerships.
